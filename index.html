Data Retention and Account Deletion

User data is retained while the account remains active or for as long as necessary to provide Molista services.

Users can delete their account directly from within the Molista app using the “Delete Account” option in account settings.

When an account is deleted:

  • Personal account information, memberships, and user-specific data are deleted.
  • Lists solely owned by the user are deleted if no other members remain.
  • If a list is shared with other members, ownership is transferred to another member instead of deleting other users' data.
  • Some shared records may remain in a de-identified form, such as displaying “Deleted Member”, to preserve the integrity of shared lists and reports.
  • User-specific invitations and personal data are removed as part of the account deletion process.
  • Local account data stored on the device, including session data and related local files, is removed.

Users may also request account deletion outside the app using the following page:

Delete Molista Account

We may retain a limited amount of information for a limited period where necessary for security, legal compliance, or abuse prevention. Once it is no longer required, the information will be deleted or de-identified.

Some information may remain in system backups for a limited period according to the technical retention cycle of the services we use.
